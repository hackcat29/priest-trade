## General

### What is Priest.trade?
Priest.trade is the Monad-native perpetuals launchpad and trading exchange. It allows any token on Monad to be created with an immediate perpetual futures market, traded with up to 40x leverage, and graduated into a real Uniswap v4 spot pool with permanent locked liquidity.

### What blockchain does Priest.trade run on?
Priest.trade runs natively on **Monad Mainnet** (Chain ID: `143`) and **Monad Testnet** (Chain ID: `10143`). It is an EVM protocol built specifically for Monad's 10,000 TPS and 1-second finality.

### What is the relationship between Perpes.fun and Priest.trade?
Perpes.fun is the Solana deployment of the protocol. Priest.trade is the standalone production brand deployed on Monad Mainnet (`priest.trade`).

## Trading

### What collateral is accepted?
All trading collateral is denominated in **native Circle USDC** on Monad (`0x754704Bc059F8C67012fEd69BC8A327a5aafb603`).

### What is the maximum leverage?
- **Warmup Markets (first 24h of a token):** Up to **10x**.
- **Active vAMM Markets:** Up to **40x**.
- **Graduated Secondary Markets:** Starts at **15x**, unlocking by 5x each day up to **40x**.

### How do liquidations work?
A position is liquidated when its equity falls below maintenance margin (1.25% at 40x leverage). Priest.trade uses a **dual-price check**: a position can only be liquidated if its margin ratio breaches the maintenance threshold at *both* the Mark Price and the Index Price, neutralizing single-block flash wicks.

### Can I set Take Profit and Stop Loss?
Yes. On-chain TP and SL triggers are stored directly in contract storage and executed automatically by protocol keepers.

## Token Launches & Listings

### Can anyone launch a token?
Yes. Token creation is permissionless. Total supply (1,000,000,000 tokens) is minted directly to the engine with zero creator pre-mine, ensuring a 100% fair launch.

### What is the difference between launching and listing?
- **Launching:** Deploys a new token that begins on the primary virtual AMM (vAMM).
- **Listing:** Connects an existing Monad token with an eligible Uniswap v3/v4 pool directly onto the secondary oracle engine.

### How much do creators earn?
Creators earn **25%** of trading fees by default, and permanently unlock **50% of trading fees** once their market reaches $100,000 in volume within 30 days.

### How does graduation work?
When a market clears 5 volume and resilience gates, the protocol seeds a Uniswap v4 spot pool with full-range liquidity and permanently burns the position NFT to `0x...dEaD`. The perp market transitions to the secondary oracle engine.

## Liquidity & Counterflow

### What is the Counterflow Vault?
Counterflow is an ERC-4626 vault (`cfUSDC`) where liquidity providers deposit USDC. Counterflow acts as the automated backstop counterparty for graduated markets, earning **30% of protocol fees** plus market surplus when traders lose.

### How do I withdraw from Counterflow?
Redemptions follow a 2-step process with a **3-day cooldown** to protect depositors from front-running losses. Once the cooldown passes, you can claim your principal and accrued yield in USDC.
