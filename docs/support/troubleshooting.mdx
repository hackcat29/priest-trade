Common issues and solutions when interacting with Priest.trade on Monad.

---

## 1. Transaction Errors

### Error: `LeverageTooHigh()`
- **Cause:** Your chosen position notional divided by collateral exceeds the market's current maximum leverage cap.
- **Solution:** Reduce your leverage multiplier or add more collateral. Note that markets in their first 24-hour **Warmup** phase are capped at **10x**.

### Error: `SlippageExceeded()` / `PriceImpactTooHigh()`
- **Cause:** Market prices moved beyond your slippage limit between order creation and block inclusion, or your order size is large relative to current virtual depth.
- **Solution:** Increase your slippage tolerance slightly in trade settings (e.g. from 1.0% to 2.0%) or split your order into smaller chunks.

### Error: `InsufficientCollateral()` / `InsufficientVaultHeadroom()`
- **Cause:** Attempting to withdraw collateral that would breach initial margin requirements, or attempting a withdrawal that exceeds available unreserved vault headroom.
- **Solution:** Keep sufficient margin in your position to maintain your health ratio, or wait for pending profits to settle.

### Error: `PendingLiquidation()`
- **Cause:** The position is already flagged for liquidation because its margin ratio fell below the maintenance margin threshold.
- **Solution:** Once flagged, positions cannot take on new collateral. Manage risk early by setting Stop Loss triggers before maintenance margin is breached.

## 2. Wallet & Connection Issues

### Wallet Doesn't Prompt on Monad
- **Cause:** Conflicting browser extensions (e.g. MetaMask and Phantom both competing for `window.ethereum`).
- **Solution:** Set Rabby or MetaMask as your default provider, or connect via Privy's embedded login. Ensure network is set to Chain ID `143` (Mainnet) or `10143` (Testnet).

### Stuck Pending Transaction / Nonce Desync
- **Cause:** Network congestion or a dropped transaction with an outdated nonce.
- **Solution:** In MetaMask, go to **Settings > Advanced > Clear activity tab data** to reset the nonce cache. Monad's 1-second blocks clear transactions rapidly once properly broadcast.

---

## 3. Position & Balance Display Issues

### Balances Not Updating Instantly
- **Cause:** Local browser RPC cache.
- **Solution:** Click the refresh icon in the upper-right corner next to your wallet address or hard refresh your browser (`Ctrl+Shift+R` or `Cmd+Shift+R`).
