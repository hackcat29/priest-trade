# Creator Dashboard

The Creator Dashboard is the central command center for token founders, community managers, and market listers.

---

## Accessing the Dashboard

1. Connect the wallet that created or listed your token.
2. In the main navigation, click **Creator** or select **Creator Dashboard** from your profile dropdown.

---

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Market Health & Analytics
- **Cumulative Volume:** Total all-time trading volume in USDC.
- **24h Volume & Trades:** Active daily engagement metrics.
- **Unique Traders:** Distinct wallet addresses that have traded your market.
- **Open Interest:** Aggregate value of currently active Long and Short positions.
- **Insurance Reserve:** Current balance in your market's on-chain insurance fund.

### 2. Graduation Progress Tracker
A real-time progress bar tracking your market's progress across the **5 Graduation Gates**:
- Gate 1: Volume ($2M Target).
- Gate 2: Trader Diversity (50+ Unique Traders, no single wallet >15%).
- Gate 3: Funding Rate Stability (<8 bps/hr average).
- Gate 4: Market Age & Resilience (7+ Days, $4,000+ Insurance).
- Gate 5: Integrity (Fair-launch verified, mint authority locked).

### 3. Creator Earnings & Claiming
- **Accrued Creator Fees:** Total USDC earned from platform trading fees waiting to be claimed.
- **Claim History:** Past fee withdrawals with transaction links.
- **Claim Button:** One-click on-chain withdrawal sending accrued fees directly to your wallet via `claimCreatorFees()`.

### 4. Community & Livestream Controls
- Start an official **Priest Room Livestream** directly from your token's trading page.
- Post pinned announcements to your market's social feed.
- Update community links (Telegram, Discord, X) as your project evolves.
