Priest.trade lets you launch a coin on Monad with an instant perpetuals market from day one, or list an existing coin without having to launch from scratch.

---

## Two ways to trade on Priest.trade

```
                          ┌───────────────────────────┐
                          │    Choose Your Pathway    │
                          └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                                        │
                ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                ▼                                               ▼
    [ Launch a New Token ]                             [ List an Existing Token ]
    • Deploys a new ERC-20 token                       • Connect an existing Uniswap pool
    • Starts on the Primary vAMM curve                 • Starts directly on the Secondary Engine
    • 10x leverage cap in 24h warmup                   • Prices off real spot depth
    • Graduates to Uniswap v4 when ready               • Backed by Counterflow Vault
```

### 1. Launch a new coin
- Create a brand-new token directly on Priest.trade.
- The protocol deploys the token contract (`PerpesToken.sol`) and initializes a vAMM perpetuals market.
- No upfront spot pool is needed; traders can long or short with leverage immediately.
- Once the coin builds volume and clears 5 gates, the protocol graduates the market by seeding a Uniswap v4 spot pool and burning the LP tokens forever.

### 2. List an existing coin
- Already have a coin trading on Monad with a Uniswap v3 or v4 pool?
- Anyone can call `listMarket()` to open an oracle-priced perp market immediately.
- The market skips the vAMM and trades directly on the secondary engine.
- The person who lists the coin earns creator trading fees!

---

## Why launch here?

1. **Earn from trading volume:** Creators earn up to **50% of all trading fees** generated by their perp market.
2. **Shorting enabled:** Your community can hedge spot holdings or profit during downturns without waiting for CEX listings.
3. **No team pre-mine rugs:** 100% of the token supply is minted directly into the engine at launch. The creator gets zero free tokens.
4. **Permanent spot liquidity:** Graduation permanently locks seed liquidity on Uniswap v4 by burning the LP position NFT to `0x...dEaD`.
