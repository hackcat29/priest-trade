## 2. Core Smart Contracts

### `PerpesEngine.sol`
The central protocol contract. It manages all perpetuals markets, accounts for per-market USDC vaults, executes vAMM and secondary orders, enforces liquidations, tracks limit orders, and manages graduations.
- **Proxy Pattern:** UUPS Upgradeable (`Initializable`, `UUPSUpgradeable`, `Ownable2StepUpgradeable`, `ReentrancyGuardUpgradeable`).
- **Storage Isolation:** Each market's deposited collateral, insurance funds, and fees are accounted for in its own `Market` struct:
  $$\text{Engine USDC Balance} = \sum(\text{vaultBalanceUsdc} + \text{orderEscrowUsdc})$$
- **Key Methods:**
  - `createTokenAndMarket(LaunchParams p)`
  - `listMarket(address token, ListingParams p)`
  - `openPosition(address token, Side side, uint256 collateral, uint256 notional, uint16 maxSlippage)`
  - `closePosition(address token, uint256 closeNotional, uint16 maxSlippage)`
  - `addCollateral(address token, uint256 amount)`
  - `removeCollateral(address token, uint256 amount)`
  - `liquidate(address token, address owner)`
  - `placeLimitOrder(address token, Side side, OrderType orderType, uint256 priceE6, uint256 collateral, uint256 notional)`
  - `cancelLimitOrder(uint256 orderId)`
  - `setTriggers(address token, uint256 takeProfitPriceE6, uint256 stopLossPriceE6)`
  - `executeTrigger(address token, address owner)`
  - `claimCreatorFees(address token)`
  - `claimProtocolFees(address token)`
  - `graduate(address token, uint256 seedUsdc, uint256 minPriceE6, uint256 maxPriceE6)`
  - `syncCounterflow(address token)`

### `CounterflowVault.sol`
The automated liquidity provider vault (`cfUSDC`).
- **Standard:** ERC-4626 tokenized vault over Circle USDC.
- **Role:** Backs secondary and graduated markets. When a market vault has a deficit paying out winners, Counterflow pays via `cover()`. Counterflow receives 30% of protocol fees and all market surplus via `onIncome()`.
- **Key Methods:**
  - `deposit(uint256 assets, address receiver)`
  - `requestRedeem(uint256 shares)`: Initiates 3-day cooldown.
  - `cancelRedeem(uint256 id)`: Cancels pending request.
  - `claimRedeem(uint256 id)`: Burns shares and withdraws assets post-cooldown.
  - `totalAssets()`: Computes conservative cash-based NAV.

### `PerpesToken.sol`
The standardized ERC-20 contract deployed for every newly launched token.
- **Fixed Supply:** 1,000,000,000 tokens (18 decimals).
- **Minting:** 100% minted to `PerpesEngine` at deployment; mint authority revoked permanently.
- **Immutable:** No freeze, pause, or blacklist capabilities.

### `PerpesOracle.sol` & `PerpesSecondaryMath.sol`
Mathematical libraries that compute on-chain Uniswap v3 TWAP (`observe`), Uniswap v4 EMA, and worst-of pricing formulas.
