The Priest.trade referral program allows traders and influencers to earn continuous cash flow by introducing new traders to the platform.

---

## 1. How the Program Works

- **Your Referral Link:** Every connected user receives a unique referral code and link (e.g. `priest.trade/ref/CONGREGATION`).
- **Lifetime Tracking:** When a friend connects their wallet using your link, their address is permanently mapped to your referral account.
- **5% Fee Rebate:** You earn **5% of every trading fee** your referred traders pay across all perpetuals markets on Priest.trade.
- **No Fee Increase for Referees:** Your referees pay the exact same low trading fees. The 5% rebate is carved out of the protocol's share as an affiliate commission.

---

## 2. Generating Your Referral Code

1. Navigate to the **Referrals** tab on [priest.trade](https://priest.trade).
2. Connect your wallet.
3. Your default referral code (derived from your address or username) will appear.
4. Click **Create Custom Code** to choose a memorable vanity slug (e.g. `ALPHA`, `WHALE`, `CHURCH`).
5. Click **Copy Link** to share on X, Telegram, or Discord.

## 4. Claiming Referral Earnings

Referral earnings accumulate in real time in your referral balance in USDC:
- View your total referred traders, total volume, and claimable balance on the dashboard.
- Click **Claim Referral Earnings** to withdraw your USDC directly to your wallet.
