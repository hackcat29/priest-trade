The Priest.trade Leaderboard showcases top-performing traders, highest volume contributors, and premier referral leaders across Monad.

---

## 1. Leaderboard Categories

You can toggle between three specialized competitive rankings:

1. **PnL Leaderboard (ROI & Absolute USD):**
   - Ranks the most profitable traders on the platform.
   - Filterable by **24 Hours**, **7 Days**, **30 Days**, or **All-Time**.
   - Highlights true trading skill and risk management.
2. **Volume Leaderboard:**
   - Ranks accounts by total notional trading volume generated.
   - Used for primary epoch reward point tier calculations.
3. **Referral Leaderboard:**
   - Recognizes top community builders and affiliate referrers based on total referred traders and referee volume.

---

## 2. Trader Profiles & Badges

Clicking any wallet row on the leaderboard opens their **Trader Profile**:
- View historical win rate, average trade duration, and top traded tokens.
- Review public badges (e.g. *Founding Trader*, *100x Club*, *Graduation Maker*, *Congregation Leader*).
- Follow their wallet to receive notifications when they enter new positions or stream in a Priest Room.
