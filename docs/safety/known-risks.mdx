This page covers known technical limits and edge cases in the protocol so you know exactly how the system behaves under extreme conditions.

---

## 1. Short buyback limits on heavily skewed curves

On the primary vAMM rail:
- If longs buy up almost all of the virtual tokens on the curve, a short trying to buy back and close can hit a temporary `PriceImpactTooHigh` error until longs reduce their positions.
- This is an inherent property of constant-product math ($x \cdot y = k$). No money can be stolen, but short exits can be delayed if long open interest is overwhelming the curve.

---

## 2. Zero liquidator bounty on deep gap moves

Liquidator rewards are paid out of a liquidated trader's remaining equity:
- If a position gaps through maintenance margin straight into negative equity, the liquidator bounty drops to zero.
- Because Priest runs dedicated protocol keeper bots, insolvent positions are closed regardless of fee bounties. However, independent third-party bots might skip liquidations that pay zero reward.

---

## 3. 60-second oracle staleness window

On graduated markets:
- Pricing relies on fresh oracle ticks from Uniswap.
- If network congestion or bot downtime delays updates past 60 seconds, new position opens and liquidations pause automatically until the price stream catches back up.

---

## 4. Permissionless listings and concentrated depth

Anyone can list an existing coin with at least $25,000 in Uniswap depth:
- On Uniswap v4, concentrated liquidity can sometimes make a pool look deeper in-range than its real total reserves.
- Because of this, newly listed coins are **not backed by Counterflow by default** until the team verifies that pool depth is authentic and well-distributed.

---

## 5. Bid wall delays during graduation

When a coin graduates:
- If someone parks a massive USDC bid wall on the Uniswap v4 pool right before graduation, the engine's price correction swap can revert with `PoolPriceMismatch`.
- If this happens, the protocol can re-route graduation to a different pool fee tier (a fresh pool key) to complete the graduation cleanly.
