Maintaining robust personal operational security (OpSec) is your primary defense when interacting with decentralized applications on Monad.

---

## 1. Protecting Your Private Keys & Seed Phrases

- **Never Share Seed Phrases:** No member of the Priest.trade team will ever ask you for your private key, seed phrase, or password. Anyone asking for these credentials is an attacker.
- **Privy Embedded Wallets:** If using social/email login with Privy, protect your underlying email and social accounts with strong unique passwords and Hardware Security Keys (YubiKey) or Authenticator App 2FA (avoid SMS 2FA).
- **Key Export Safety:** If exporting your private key from Privy to an external extension like Rabby or MetaMask, do so on a private, malware-free machine.

---

## 2. Token Approval Hygiene

When trading or using Counterflow:
- You will approve the `PerpesEngine` or `CounterflowVault` contract to transfer your USDC.
- **Verify Contract Addresses:** Always check that the contract you are approving matches the official deployments listed in [Contract Deployments](/developers/deployments).
- **Revoke Allowances:** If taking an extended break from trading, use tools like Revoke.cash on Monad to reset unused token approvals to zero.

---

## 3. Phishing & Domain Verification

- **Official Domain:** Always verify that the URL in your browser address bar is exactly:
  `https://priest.trade`
- Beware of phishing domains with subtle typos (e.g. `prlest.trade`, `priest-trade.com`).
- Bookmark the official site and access it exclusively through your bookmarks.
- Never click unverified direct links sent to you in Telegram or Discord direct messages (DMs).
