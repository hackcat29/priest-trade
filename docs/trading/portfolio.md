# Portfolio & Trade History

The Portfolio dashboard provides comprehensive accounting of your trading performance, active capital allocations, and historical transactions.

---

## 1. Portfolio Overview

Navigate to the **Portfolio** tab in the main navigation to view:

- **Total Equity:** The sum of all active position collateral plus net unrealized PnL across all markets.
- **Available Margin:** Free USDC in your wallet ready to be deployed.
- **Active Margin:** Total USDC currently locked as collateral in open positions.
- **Escrowed Balance:** USDC locked in resting limit orders.
- **Unsettled Profits:** Claimable profits from secondary market closes awaiting withdrawal.
- **All-Time Realized PnL:** Aggregate lifetime profit or loss realized across all closed positions.
- **Win Rate:** Percentage of closed positions that exited with net profit.

---

## 2. Active Positions Breakdown

A multi-market table showing all your currently open trades across Monad:
- View risk distribution across meme coins, graduated markets, and listed assets.
- Rapidly adjust collateral on positions close to liquidation.
- Close individual positions or click **Close All** in emergencies.

---

## 3. Trade History & Performance Logs

The **Trade History** tab presents a chronological log of all on-chain actions:
- **Timestamp & Transaction Hash:** Direct links to the MonadVision block explorer.
- **Action Type:** `OPEN_LONG`, `OPEN_SHORT`, `CLOSE`, `LIQUIDATE`, `ADD_COLLATERAL`, `REMOVE_COLLATERAL`.
- **Execution Price & Size:** Volume and entry/exit pricing.
- **Realized PnL & Fees Paid:** Exact accounting for tax reporting and strategy optimization.
- **Export Data:** Download your complete trade ledger as a CSV file.
