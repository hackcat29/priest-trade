Funding rates are regular payments exchanged between traders to keep the perp contract price in line with the spot price.

---

## How funding works

Funding does not go to Priest.trade. It flows **directly between longs and shorts**:

- **Perp price is HIGHER than spot (premium):**
  - Funding rate is **positive**.
  - **Longs pay shorts.**
  - This makes longing more expensive and rewards shorting, nudging the price back down toward spot.
- **Perp price is LOWER than spot (discount):**
  - Funding rate is **negative**.
  - **Shorts pay longs.**
  - This makes shorting more expensive and rewards longing, nudging the price back up toward spot.

---

## Continuous settlement (no 8-hour cliff)

Most centralized exchanges settle funding once every 8 hours. If you close your trade 1 minute before the bell, you pay nothing; if you enter 1 minute before, you pay the full amount.

On Priest.trade:
- Funding accrues **second by second**.
- It settles automatically whenever you open, increase, reduce, or close your trade.
- You only pay or earn funding for the exact minutes and seconds your position was actually open.

---

## Funding caps

To prevent wild funding spikes from draining traders during brief market wicks:
- Funding velocity is capped per interval.
- Total funding per period cannot exceed the contract rate cap (typically 1.0% per interval).
- If trading volume or open interest is near zero, funding accumulation pauses so artificial debt doesn't build up on dormant markets.
