The insurance fund is an on-chain safety reserve. Its job is to absorb losses when a liquidated position falls below zero equity before it can be closed.

---

## Isolated per market

Instead of one giant pool where an exploit on a micro-cap coin could drain funds from everyone else, Priest.trade keeps **a separate insurance reserve for each individual market**.

- What happens in one meme coin market stays in that market.
- Big, liquid markets build up their own independent safety nets.
- You can inspect any market's insurance balance on-chain at any time.

---

## Where the money comes from

A market's insurance reserve grows in three ways:

1. **10% of all trade fees:** Every time someone opens or closes a trade on that coin, 10% of the platform fee goes directly into that market's insurance reserve.
2. **Liquidation penalties:** When a position is liquidated with equity left over, a portion of the remainder goes into the reserve.
3. **Pre-graduation pool corrections:** If someone tries to mess with the Uniswap pool price right before graduation, the engine's rebalancing swap extracts the difference and sends it to the insurance reserve.

---

## When it gets used

If a market moves so fast that a trader's position gets liquidated at a price worse than zero equity (bad debt):

1. The market's insurance reserve pays the shortfall.
2. If the insurance reserve is emptied on a graduated market, the **Counterflow Vault** steps in to cover the rest.
3. Solvent traders never take haircuts or auto-deleveraging under normal conditions.

---

## Headroom cap

The contract guarantees that insurance money cannot be drained by ordinary payouts:
- You can only withdraw collateral or claim trading profits from surplus headroom — never from funds tagged for insurance or unpaid creator fees.
