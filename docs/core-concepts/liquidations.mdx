A liquidation happens when a position runs out of margin. If your losses get too close to your deposited collateral, the protocol closes your trade to prevent you from going into negative balance.

---

## When does liquidation happen?

Your position gets liquidated when your **Margin Ratio** drops below the **Maintenance Margin** requirement:

- At **40x leverage**, your maintenance margin is **1.25%**.
- That means if your remaining equity (collateral plus unrealized PnL minus owed funding) drops to 1.25% of your position's total value, your position is liquidated.

---

## Protection against wick liquidations

On volatile meme coins, sudden flash wicks on one exchange or pool can wipe people out unfairly.

Priest.trade uses a **dual-price check** to stop this:
- Your position is only liquidated if your margin ratio is breached at **both the mark price AND the index price** (the smoothed moving average).
- If someone pumps or dumps the price for a single block, the smoothed index barely moves, and the liquidation transaction reverts. You stay in your trade.

---

## What happens during a liquidation

When a position is liquidated:

1. The position is closed at the market price through the curve or secondary engine.
2. Any remaining equity is split:
   - A trading fee is deducted.
   - A small reward goes to the keeper bot that executed the liquidation.
   - A penalty goes to the market's insurance fund.
   - Whatever is left over goes back to your wallet.
3. If the position closed with negative equity (a gap down past zero), the market's insurance fund absorbs the loss.

---

## How to avoid getting liquidated

- **Use Stop Losses:** Set an automatic stop loss on your trade so you exit before reaching maintenance margin.
- **Add Margin:** If a trade is moving against you, click the `+` icon next to your position to add more USDC collateral. This moves your liquidation price farther away.
- **Trade with Lower Leverage:** High leverage leaves almost no room for error. A 10x position gives you a ~9% buffer, while 40x gives you only ~1.25%.
