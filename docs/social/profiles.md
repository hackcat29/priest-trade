# Profiles

Every connected trader on Priest.trade has a personalized, public on-chain profile.

---

## 1. Setting Up Your Profile

1. Click your wallet address in the top-right corner and select **Edit Profile**.
2. **Display Name & Handle:** Choose a unique username.
3. **Bio:** Share your trading philosophy (Default: *"New to the congregation"*).
4. **Avatar:** Upload a custom profile photo or select from curated Monad-themed avatars.
5. **Social Verification:** Connect your X (Twitter) or Telegram account to verify your public identity.

---

## 2. Public Statistics & Transparency

Your profile showcases your verifiable trading track record:
- **Lifetime Realized PnL:** Total USD profit or loss generated across all closed positions.
- **Win / Loss Ratio:** Visual pie chart showing profitable vs unprofitable trades.
- **Most Traded Assets:** Your top markets by volume.
- **Account Age & Badges:** Distinct honors earned during platform participation.

---

## 3. Privacy Controls

If you prefer to trade anonymously:
- You can toggle **Incognito Mode** in your settings.
- In Incognito mode, your exact dollar trade sizes and net balances are masked with asterisks (`***`), displaying only percentage returns on public feeds and leaderboards.
