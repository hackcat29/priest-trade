# Priest Room Livestreams

Priest.trade integrates native real-time audio and video livestreams directly into the trading terminal, powered by our ultra-low-latency **LiveKit** infrastructure.

---

## 1. What is a Priest Room?

A **Priest Room** is an interactive live broadcast where token founders, community hosts, or guest analysts stream video and audio directly on a market's trading page:

- **Watch While Trading:** The video feed docks seamlessly above or beside the candlestick chart. You can watch the host, listen to community alpha, and execute leveraged trades simultaneously without switching windows.
- **Sub-Second Latency:** Powered by LiveKit WebRTC edge networks, video and audio stream with less than 200ms latency, enabling real-time Q&A and coordinated community trading.
- **Integrated Trade Flares:** When a viewer executes a trade while watching a stream, an animated trade banner flares across the stream screen, celebrating community conviction!

---

## 2. Tuning Into a Stream

1. Browse the **Markets** page or look for the flashing **LIVE** badge on the navigation bar.
2. Click any live market row to open the trading terminal.
3. The video player will automatically connect to the active Priest Room.
4. Adjust volume, enter full-screen mode, or pop out the video player to a floating picture-in-picture window.

---

## 3. Hosting a Stream as a Creator

If you launched or listed a token, hosting livestreams is the most powerful way to build community conviction:

1. Connect the creator wallet associated with your market.
2. In the market header or Creator Dashboard, click **Go Live**.
3. Allow browser access to your camera and microphone (or configure OBS via RTMP ingest).
4. Enter your stream title (e.g. *Monad Town Hall & Perp Trading Session*).
5. Click **Start Broadcast**.
6. All traders browsing your market will immediately receive a live notification to tune in!
