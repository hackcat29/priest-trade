Celebrate winning trades and share your market conviction with customized, high-resolution **PnL Share Cards**.

---

## 1. Generating a PnL Card

1. Navigate to the **Positions** table or your **Trade History**.
2. On any open or closed trade, click the **Share** (PnL Card) icon.
3. The PnL card preview modal will open, displaying:
   - Token Ticker and Direction (`LONG` or `SHORT`).
   - Entry Price, Exit / Mark Price, and Leverage Multiplier.
   - Return on Investment percentage (e.g. `+142.5%`).
   - Verifiable QR code linking directly to the transaction on MonadVision.

---

## 3. Exporting & Sharing

- **Copy Image:** Copies the rendered image directly to your clipboard for instant pasting into Telegram, Discord, or X.
- **Download PNG:** Saves a high-resolution 1200x675 image optimized for social media share previews.
- **Share to X (Twitter):** One-click button that drafts a tweet with your trade stats and your custom referral link automatically attached!
