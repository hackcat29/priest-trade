Accounting in `CounterflowVault.sol` is designed to be cash-based, conservative, and resistant to front-running.

---

## 1. Conservative NAV Calculation

In standard DeFi vaults, Net Asset Value (NAV) often counts paper profits or projected debt as assets, leading to inaccurate valuations when markets turn.

Counterflow enforces **Cash-Based NAV Accounting**:

$$\text{NAV} = \text{totalAssets()} = \text{Cash} - \text{Locked Profit}$$

Where:
- **Cash:** The actual physical USDC balance held by the `CounterflowVault` contract (`IERC20(usdc).balanceOf(address(this))`).
- **Locked Profit:** Newly swept income that has not yet unlocked over the 1-day vesting period.
- **Unpaid Covers:** When Counterflow covers a market deficit, that capital is written off as an immediate realized loss the moment it leaves the vault. What the engine owes back is **not counted** as an asset until it physically arrives.

---

## 2. The 1-Day Linear Profit Unlock

When a market sweeps trading fees or trader losses into Counterflow via `syncCounterflow()`, that income does not immediately boost the share price in a single block.

### Why Profit Unlocking?
If a large surplus sweep immediately bumped the share price by 5%, an MEV searcher could deposit right before the sweep transaction and withdraw right after, diluting existing LPs.

### The Linear Unlocking Mechanism:
- When income arrives, it is added to `lockedProfit`.
- The profit unlocks smoothly and linearly over **24 hours** (`profitUnlockSeconds = 86,400\text{ seconds}`).
- Every second, a fraction of that profit vests permanently into NAV:
  $$\text{Current Locked Profit} = \frac{\text{Locked Profit} \times (\text{Unlock Window} - \text{Elapsed})}{\text{Unlock Window}}$$
- Existing long-term depositors capture 100% of the yield smoothly.

## 3. Revenue Sources & Fee Streams

Counterflow generates yield through two continuous mechanisms:

### A. 30% Protocol Fee Share
Every trade executed on a Counterflow-backed market pays a 0.10% fee. Exactly **30% of the protocol's fee cut** is automatically routed to Counterflow. As long as trading occurs on Monad, Counterflow earns passive cash flow.

### B. Trader Net Losses (Surplus Sweeps)
Perpetuals markets are naturally zero-sum before fees. When traders lose on a backed market, the market collateral vault accumulates a cash surplus beyond what is needed to back existing open interest:
$$\text{Surplus} = \text{Vault} - \text{Reserved} - \text{Collateral} - \text{Buffer (10%)} - \text{Positive PnL}$$
Anyone can permissionlessly call `syncCounterflow(token)` on the engine. The surplus is transferred directly to the vault:
- It **repays past covers** first.
- The remainder becomes net profit that begins unlocking over 24 hours.
